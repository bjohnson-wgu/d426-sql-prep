Informed Consent & Research Participation Agreement

Please review the following study information carefully before deciding whether to participate.

Study Title: Learning Preferences and Course Sequencing in Online Database Education

Researcher: Bruce C. Johnson III, Instructor, Faculty Lead, School of Technology (b.johnson1@wgu.edu)

Institutional Non-Sponsorship Disclosure:
This study is an independent academic research project conducted by Bruce Johnson, Instructor, Faculty Lead, School of Technology (b.johnson1@wgu.edu). It is NOT sponsored, funded, or required by Western Governors University (WGU).

1. Purpose of the Study: You are invited to take part in an educational research study examining whether starting with hands-on SQL query mechanics before reading abstract database theory influences study velocity and objective assessment outcomes in D426.

2. Age & Eligibility Requirement: You must be at least 18 years of age to participate in this study. If you are under 18 years of age, you are not eligible to take part.

3. Completely Voluntary Participation & Zero Grade Impact: Your participation is 100% voluntary. You may choose not to participate, or discontinue at any time. Opting out will have no effect on your course grade, academic standing, tuition, progress pacing, or access to course materials and faculty coaching.

4. Study Procedures & Time Commitment: If you consent to participate, you will complete a quick study preference check-in (~3 minutes). Depending on random group assignment, you may also receive optional access to a 5-module interactive SQL practice sandbox (~25 to 30 minutes). The optional SQL practice activities are designed for standard laptop and desktop web browsers.

5. Privacy & Data Handling: Your invitation link uses a pre-masked random study token instead of your WGU student ID, so your name, email, and institutional student ID never enter this website. Completion timestamps and survey responses are linked offline only to match course milestones, and all results are reported strictly in group summaries.

6. Contact Information: If you have questions about the study, contact Bruce Johnson at b.johnson1@wgu.edu. For questions regarding your rights as a participant, contact the WGU Institutional Review Board at irb@wgu.edu.

About This Practice Sandbox & Exploratory Capabilities
This interactive environment runs a lightweight SQL engine directly inside your web browser so you can practice core D426 commands (CREATE TABLE, INSERT, UPDATE, SELECT, DESCRIBE, and INNER JOIN) with zero setup. Feel free to run custom test queries in the editor at any time; advanced server administration features such as stored procedures, triggers, and user permissions are outside the scope of this lightweight practice sandbox.

Welcome to the D426 interactive SQL practice sandbox! Before diving into abstract database theory, you will complete five short, hands-on modules covering creating tables, adding data, updating rows, querying records, and joining tables. Watch the short clip on the left, edit or run the starter SQL in the editor on the right, and if you ever hit a syntax error, a targeted troubleshooting tip and walkthrough video will open automatically. Your completed modules save automatically in your browser, so feel free to pause and return anytime using your personal link.
