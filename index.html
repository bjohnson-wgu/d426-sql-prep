Read Welcome Tour Transcript

Hey there, and welcome to the D426 SQL Sandbox! First off, thank you so much for jumping in and participating in this research study—I’m genuinely excited you’re here. When you’re starting out in Data Management Foundations, diving straight into dense textbook theory can feel a little overwhelming. Spending just about 25 to 30 minutes getting your hands dirty with a few of the most common, high-impact SQL commands right up front helps you hit the ground running. Across the top of the screen, you’ll see five modules: creating tables, adding data, updating data, querying data, and joining tables together. Each module includes a short walkthrough video on the left and a live MySQL editor on the right with starter code ready to run. Feel free to experiment or even make a mistake on purpose to unlock the troubleshooting drawer and bonus tip videos—and click 'Reset to Template' anytime to restore the original code. Click 'Let's Get Started' whenever you're ready!
