Study Title: Learning Preferences and Course Sequencing in Online Database Education

Researcher: Bruce Johnson, Instructor, Faculty Lead, School of Technology (b.johnson1@wgu.edu)

Notice Regarding WGU Sponsorship:
This research study is being conducted independently by instructor Bruce Johnson. This study is NOT sponsored, funded, or required by Western Governors University (WGU).

1. What is this study about? We are studying how the order of course topics affects learning in D426 (Data Management Foundations). Specifically, we are testing whether starting with SQL coding practice before reading database theory affects the time students spend on the course and how they do on their final exam.

2. Age Requirement: You must be at least 18 years old to take part. If you are under 18, you cannot participate in this study.

3. Your Choice (Voluntary): Taking part is 100% voluntary. You can choose not to join, or you can stop at any time. Choosing not to participate will have no effect on your course grade, academic progress, tuition, or your access to instructors, cohorts, and study resources.

4. What will you do and time commitment? If you agree, you will complete a brief 3-minute questionnaire regarding your study habits and prior technical experience. Depending on your randomly assigned study group, you may also receive optional access to an interactive, self-paced SQL practice sandbox (approximately 15–20 minutes). In total, participating represents a commitment of approximately 20–25 minutes (or just 3 minutes if assigned to the standard study path). You may pause, close your browser, and return at any time.

5. Privacy & Your Information: We use your student ID solely to match your survey responses with your course progress. Before analyzing any data, your name and ID will be removed and replaced with a random code. Only group summary numbers will ever be reported or published.

6. Questions & Contacts: If you have questions about the study, contact Bruce Johnson at b.johnson1@wgu.edu. For questions regarding your rights as a research participant, contact the WGU Institutional Review Board at irb@wgu.edu.

Sandbox Scope & Exploratory Capabilities
This practice environment is a client-side WebAssembly emulator configured specifically for the core DDL, DML, and DQL operations taught in D426 (table definitions, constraints, updates, filters, and standard inner joins). It does not emulate an entire production MySQL server or support advanced server routines (e.g., triggers, stored procedures, or administrative commands). For unrestricted multi-table database design or advanced dialect experimentation, we recommend using a full online engine like DB Fiddle.
