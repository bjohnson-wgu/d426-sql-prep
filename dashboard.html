Complete Study Guide & Metric Data Dictionary (Click to Expand) Plain-Language Reference for All Stakeholders

This dashboard monitors operational health and student engagement for the independent D426 Curricular Sequencing Pilot. All records are keyed strictly to randomly generated S26_ tokens—no student names, email addresses, grades, or institutional IDs are stored in this system.

1. Study Groups & Recruitment Funnel

  • Control Group (CTRL): Students assigned to the standard D426 course sequence. After the brief check-in, they go directly to their regular D426 course shell and textbook.
  • Intervention Group (INTERV): Students offered optional access to the 5-module interactive SQL practice sandbox before starting their standard D426 textbook chapters.
  • Invited: Total unique students sent a study invitation email (tracked via INVITED).
  • Links Opened: Unique students who clicked their personalized link and loaded the consent page (LINK_OPENED).
  • Consented (Agreed vs. Declined): Voluntary participation decision on Screen 1A. Students under 18 or preferring not to join select "NO" (CONSENT_DECLINED) and proceed straight to standard course resources.
  • Screener Completed: Consented students who submitted the 12-question baseline SQL & study preference check-in (DIAGNOSTIC_COMPLETED).

2. Sandbox Progress & Time Metrics

  • Self-Paced Design (No Time Limit): Students may pause, close their browser, or return anytime using their personal link. No cutoff or timer restriction is ever imposed.
  • Active Time (Minutes): Measured only while the browser tab is visible and the user has interacted within the past 2 minutes. Idle or background tabs are automatically excluded.
  • Full vs. Partial Completion: Full Completion means passing all 5 SQL exercises (STEP_5_COMPLETED). Partial Activity means starting or passing 1–4 exercises without finishing Module 5 yet.
  • Attempt & Tip Events: Each module logs code runs, syntax/validation checks (STEP_X_ATTEMPT_FAIL), and whether the student opened the troubleshooting drawer or revealed the starter solution after 3 tries.

3. Video Engagement & Exit Pathways

  • 11 Embedded Micro-Videos: Includes 1 Welcome Tour video (TOUR), 5 primary module walkthroughs (1.1–5.1), and 5 troubleshooting tip videos (1.2–5.2).
  • Videos Started vs. Completed: VIDEO_STARTED fires when playback begins; VIDEO_COMPLETED fires when a video reaches its end (counted accurately even if watched at 1.5x or 2x speed).
  • Pathway A vs. Pathway B: Upon finishing Module 5, students choose either Pathway A (EXIT_PATHWAY_A: jump straight into zyBooks Chapter 2 SQL coding) or Pathway B (EXIT_PATHWAY_B: start at Chapter 1 database theory).
  • Session Exit / Pause: SESSION_PAUSE and SESSION_EXIT record where a student leaves off if they switch tabs or close the browser before clicking a pathway link.

4. Baseline Survey & Privacy Identifiers

  • Prior SQL Familiarity (1–5): Self-reported experience before starting D426 (1 = None, 2 = Very little, 3 = Moderate, 4 = Comfortable, 5 = Professional). Levels 1–2 are grouped as "Novice / No Prior SQL" in the executive summary.
  • ILS Learning Preference: Based on the 11-item Felder-Soloman Sequential/Global scale (scored from −11 to +11). Positive scores (+1 to +11) indicate a Sequential preference (step-by-step); negative scores (−1 to −11) indicate a Global preference (big-picture first).
  • Study Token & Session Hash: S26_XXXXXX links events for the same participant anonymously; sess_... is a temporary browser-tab ID used only to detect multi-session returns.
